# Codex Launch Video — Teardown & Rebuild Brief

Deep analysis of the Codex-generated 43s Ecelon launch video (6-lens
adversarial critique of the extracted frames + audio), and a corrected
rebuild plan + ready-to-paste Codex prompt.

## Verdict

The Codex cut is a mislabeled feature tour, not a launch film. It opens on ~2s of dead black, then dumps straight into product mechanics with no problem, no hook, and no reason to care; it names the entire product after "Nova" — a throwaway test-agent string the user once typed — plastering it across every eyebrow, headline and tagline as if that were the brand; and it rides a robotic, monotone AI voiceover whose flat RMS (0.10-0.18 the whole way) means nothing builds and the CTA lands with zero lift. Worse, it reads as static screenshots because the only transition is a cross-dissolve that literally double-exposes whole screens (Fleet Overview printed on top of Activity; wizard step titles colliding), nothing animates in, the two hero "intelligence in motion" assets (node graph, price chart) don't move, and the trust differentiators that would actually sell it — custody, explainability, discipline — are buried past the midpoint behind a 19-second form. This needs a problem-led rebuild with real motion, faithful product chrome, a music-and-kinetic-type track, and one locked dataset — not a tweak.

## Top problems (ranked)

1. No hook, no problem, dead-air open. The first ~2s are near-black with two dots, then it cuts straight to 'Name your agent' UI. The highest-bounce window states no trader pain and gives the viewer nothing to feel or want.
2. 'Nova' masquerades as the product. A throwaway test-agent name is promoted into eyebrows, headlines, breadcrumbs, taglines and status copy on nearly every screen (BUILD NOVA, NOVA IS LIVE, NOVA MANIFEST, 'Nova is watching'), so the film reads as a mascot launch for the wrong thing — and 'Nova is watching' is tonally ominous for a custody-first finance tool.
3. Robotic wall-to-wall AI voiceover with no energy arc. This is the user's #1 complaint and the wrong medium for a ~1-cut-per-second UI launch; flat RMS proves monotone, over-normalized TTS rushed across ~14 screens, and the CTA sits at the exact same level as the setup so there is no payoff.
4. Cross-dissolve is the only transition and it double-exposes entire screens. Fleet Overview renders on top of a still-legible Activity log; 'Describe the strategy'/'Set the risk'/'Allocate capital' step titles and eyebrows print over each other mid-transition. It looks both static AND broken.
5. Nothing animates. Every card, panel and stat is fully composed the instant its scene starts; the node graph (the one screen built for signature motion) is a still diagram with no edges drawing or data flowing; count-ups crawl linearly like a loading bar. There is no 'intelligence in motion.'
6. WHY is inverted behind HOW. The 19s form-fill wizard eats ~44% of runtime before any reason to believe; the trust pillars (funds never leave your exchange, every decision explained, shows restraint) are scattered and back-loaded past the midpoint, and the emotional climax ('NOVA IS LIVE') lands at 49% with 22s of flat feature cards after it.
7. Invented UI instead of the real product. Codex fabricated an eyebrow+headline slide template, fake section names, and taglines ('Intelligence in motion', 'LIVE COMPILER', 'Perpetual', 'AGENT STATUS') that don't exist, while omitting the real chrome (nav rail, page pill, agent sub-nav), the Step-1 agent-type choice, and the entire Trading Graph hero screen.
8. Wrong, contradictory data with zero-states as hero numbers. Capital morphs 60,938 -> 100,000 -> 33,008; confidence floor is both 15 and 72; the 'here's what your agents did' payoff shows +0.00%, 'No trade', and 0 open positions — the climax screen shows the agent doing nothing.
9. Passive, low-desire CTA. The most important beat is 'watch a browser silently type a URL' over a logo, with no energy peak, no restated promise, and no benefit-anchored ask — landing on an audience the video never gave momentum.
10. Palette violations. Green and red are used for P&L/LONG/LIVE/Emergency/FLAG, breaking the one explicitly locked brand decision (strict white/orange/near-black, no green/red).
